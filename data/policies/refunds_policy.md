# Refunds Policy

Once a returned item is received and inspected, refunds are issued to the original payment method within 3-5 business days. It can take an additional 2-10 business days for the refund to appear on your statement, depending on your bank or card issuer.

If you paid with a gift card or store credit, the refund is issued back to that balance instead, usually within 24 hours.

Shipping charges are refunded only if the return is due to our error (wrong item shipped, item arrived damaged or defective) or if the item was cancelled before it shipped. Otherwise, original shipping fees are non-refundable.

If 10 business days have passed since you received a refund confirmation email and the funds still haven't appeared, contact your bank first, then reach out to us if it's still not resolved.
