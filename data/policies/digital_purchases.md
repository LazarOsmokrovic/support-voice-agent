# Digital Purchases

Digital items — e-books, downloadable software, digital gift cards, and streaming rentals or purchases — are non-returnable and non-refundable once the download or access has started, except where required by law.

If a digital purchase fails to download or is defective (corrupted file, wrong item delivered), contact us within 14 days for a replacement or refund.

Digital purchases are charged immediately at checkout, unlike physical orders which are charged at shipment.
