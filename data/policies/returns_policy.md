# Returns Policy

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund, as long as they're in the condition you received them — unused, undamaged, and with any original tags or seals intact.

A small number of categories have different rules: opened software, digital downloads, and gift cards are not eligible for return at all (see the Digital Purchases policy).

Items marked "Final Sale" at checkout cannot be returned or exchanged for any reason. If you're not sure whether an item qualifies, check the product page or ask before purchasing.

To start a return, go to Your Orders and select "Return or replace items" next to the order in question. You'll be asked for a reason for the return and given a prepaid shipping label for eligible items.
