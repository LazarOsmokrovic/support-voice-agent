# Payment Methods

We accept major credit and debit cards, PayPal, and store gift cards. Some regions also support additional local payment methods, shown at checkout if available.

Your card is only charged when your order ships, not when it's placed — except for digital purchases and pre-orders, which are charged immediately or at release, respectively.

If a payment fails, we'll hold your order for 24 hours to give you a chance to update your payment method before it's automatically cancelled.

We do not store full card numbers; only the card network and last 4 digits are kept on file for your reference in Your Orders.
