# Lost or Stolen Packages

If tracking shows your package as delivered but you can't find it, first check with neighbors and around your delivery location, since carriers sometimes leave packages in a slightly different spot. Give it 48 hours, as some carriers mark packages delivered slightly before drop-off.

If it still hasn't turned up after 48 hours, contact us with your order number and we'll open a claim with the carrier and typically send a free replacement or refund while that's in progress.

Packages confirmed lost in transit (never marked delivered) can be reported any time after the estimated delivery window has passed.

We're not able to replace packages more than once per order for repeat "not received" claims on the same address without additional verification, to prevent abuse.
