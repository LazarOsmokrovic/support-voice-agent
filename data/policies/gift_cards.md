# Gift Cards

Gift cards never expire and have no fees. Balances can be checked at any time on the Gift Cards page after logging in.

Gift cards can be combined with one other payment method at checkout but cannot be used to purchase other gift cards. Physical gift cards can be redeemed online by entering the code on the back; digital gift cards are emailed with a redemption code.

Lost or stolen gift card codes cannot be refunded or replaced unless you have the original purchase receipt or order confirmation.
