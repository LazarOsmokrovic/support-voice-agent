# Price Adjustments

If an item you purchased drops in price within 7 days of your order date, we'll refund the difference to your original payment method — just contact us with your order number.

Price adjustments don't apply to items bought during a site-wide sale event (like a holiday sale) if the price later returns to its regular price, or to clearance and Final Sale items.

Only one price adjustment is allowed per item per order, and it must be requested within the 7-day window — we're not able to issue adjustments after that.
