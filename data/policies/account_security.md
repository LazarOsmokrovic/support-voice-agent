# Account Security

If you notice a purchase you didn't make, change your password immediately and contact us — we'll investigate and refund any unauthorized charges once confirmed.

We will never call, email, or message you asking for your full password, one-time verification code, or full card number. Treat any such request as a scam attempt and don't respond to it.

You can enable two-step verification in your account settings for extra protection; we strongly recommend it if you save a payment method to your account.

Password reset links expire after 30 minutes for security. If yours expires, just request a new one.
