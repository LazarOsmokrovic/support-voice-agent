# Order Cancellations

Orders can be cancelled for free as long as they haven't entered the "Preparing for Shipment" stage — check Your Orders for the current status. Once an order ships, it can no longer be cancelled; you'll need to refuse delivery or request a return instead.

Some items, including custom or personalized products and digital downloads, cannot be cancelled once the order is placed, since processing begins immediately.

To cancel an eligible order, go to Your Orders and select "Cancel items." A cancellation confirmation email will be sent once it's processed; if you don't receive one within an hour, the order may have already begun shipping.
