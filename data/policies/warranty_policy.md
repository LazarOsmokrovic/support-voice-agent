# Warranty Policy

Most electronics and appliances sold on our store carry a 1-year manufacturer's warranty covering defects in materials or workmanship under normal use. Warranty terms vary by manufacturer and are listed on the product page.

Extended warranty plans (2 or 3 years) can be purchased separately at checkout for eligible items and cover the same defects as the manufacturer warranty, plus one accidental damage claim.

Warranties do not cover damage from misuse, unauthorized repairs, normal wear and tear, or cosmetic damage that doesn't affect function.

To file a warranty claim, contact us with your order number and a description of the issue. Depending on the manufacturer, we'll either issue a replacement directly or connect you with the manufacturer's service center.
