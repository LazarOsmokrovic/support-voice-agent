# Shipping Policy

Standard shipping takes 3-5 business days and is free on orders over $25. Orders under $25 have a flat $5.99 standard shipping fee.

Expedited shipping (1-2 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee that varies by order weight and destination. Orders placed before 2 PM local time ship the same business day when expedited shipping is selected; orders placed after that ship the next business day.

We don't ship on weekends or major holidays, so a "3-5 business day" estimate placed on a Friday won't include that weekend.

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number by email. Delivery estimates are just that — estimates — and can shift due to weather, carrier delays, or high shipping volume around holidays.
