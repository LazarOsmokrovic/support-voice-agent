# International Shipping

We currently ship to over 45 countries outside the continental US. International orders typically take 7-21 business days to arrive, depending on the destination and customs processing times, which are outside of our control.

Customers are responsible for any customs duties, import taxes, or brokerage fees charged by the destination country. These are not included in the order total at checkout and are collected by the carrier or local customs authority on delivery.

Some products — including certain electronics, batteries, and liquids — cannot be shipped internationally due to shipping regulations. If an item in your cart isn't eligible, you'll see a notice before checkout.

International orders are not eligible for expedited shipping and cannot be redirected to a different address once shipped.
