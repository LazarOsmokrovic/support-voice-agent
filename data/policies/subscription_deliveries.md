# Subscription Deliveries

Subscription deliveries let you set up recurring orders for everyday items (like household supplies) at a discount, shipped automatically on a schedule you choose — every 1, 2, 3, or 6 months.

You can skip, delay, or cancel an upcoming subscription delivery any time before it enters processing, from the Subscriptions page in your account. Changes made after processing begins will apply to the next scheduled delivery instead.

Subscription orders are charged a few days before each shipment so you have time to make changes. The discount only applies while an active subscription is in place for that item.

If a subscribed item becomes unavailable, we'll notify you by email before the next scheduled charge rather than substituting a different product automatically.
