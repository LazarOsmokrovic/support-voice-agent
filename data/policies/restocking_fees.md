# Restocking Fees

Most returns have no restocking fee. Large items like furniture, major appliances, and TVs over 50 inches may carry a restocking fee of up to 15% if returned for reasons other than damage or defect — this is disclosed on the product page before purchase.

No restocking fee applies if the item arrived damaged, defective, or different from what was ordered.

Opened software and opened media (unless defective) are not eligible for return at all, so no restocking fee applies — they simply can't be returned.
